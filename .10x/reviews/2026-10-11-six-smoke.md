## What the earlier runs found (all in the smoke, none in the server)

- The client sat again on every welcome; the server welcomes again after a sit, so it looped into the frame limit (4429). Now it sits from a welcome that says it has no seat, once per connection.
- A player "timing out" ignored every turn of its hand, so it timed out twice and was sat out. It now leaves exactly one decision to the clock.
- A sit sent while the socket was reconnecting was lost; the welcome on return now sits it again.
- Quick-sit could land on a table from an earlier run. The smoke now names occupied tables as tried, so the lobby opens a fresh one.
